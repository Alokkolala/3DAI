Concept cheatsheet

  • Gradient descent: adjusts weights opposite the gradient to minimize loss.
  • Learning rate: step size; too high diverges, too low is slow.
  • Overfitting: model memorizes noise. Watch for low loss but stagnant accuracy.
  • Decision boundary: surface where model is uncertain (prediction = 0.5).
  • Batch size: balances stability vs. exploration; small batches add stochasticity.
